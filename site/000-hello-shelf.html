Episode Zero: Testing the Machine

A sixty-second smoke test of the Annotated Shelf pipeline.

23 August 2026

Hello, and welcome to the Annotated Shelf. If you are hearing this in a podcast app, then the entire pipeline works: a text file was pushed to a repository, a machine in a data center somewhere rendered this narration, updated a feed, and your app collected it — all without any laptop being awake.

Which is, come to think of it, a fitting first test for a series about large systems doing things no individual person quite did. Somewhere between the commit and your ears, there was nobody to blame, and for once that's good news.

Episode one, on Robert Jackall's Moral Mazes, is next. Thanks for listening.
